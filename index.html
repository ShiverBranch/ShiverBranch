ABOUT THE GAME

Primal Dread is an atmospheric Gorilla Tag horror fan game developed by Shiver Branch. Explore abandoned forests, haunted caves, forgotten facilities, and survive terrifying creatures while uncovering the mysteries hidden beneath the world.

THE STORY

Strange disappearances have been reported deep within the forest. What began as a simple investigation quickly becomes a fight for survival. Every tunnel leads somewhere. Every sound means something. And not everything hiding in the dark was ever human.
